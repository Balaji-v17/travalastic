TRAVALASTIC — COMPLETE LUXURY PRODUCT DESIGN TRANSFORMATION

You are redesigning Travalastic as a premium, high-trust travel platform for affluent and upper-premium travelers.

This is NOT a cosmetic redesign.

Do not simply change colors, add gradients, increase border-radius, replace icons, or make existing cards prettier.

Re-evaluate the entire product experience and redesign the interface architecture wherever necessary.

The final product should feel closer to a private travel concierge / luxury travel house than a generic OTA, booking marketplace, or travel SaaS dashboard.

CORE POSITIONING

Travalastic should communicate:

• effortless
• intelligent
• discreet
• premium
• curated
• highly personalized
• trustworthy
• calm
• editorial
• sophisticated

The interface should never feel:
• cheap
• crowded
• overly colorful
• generic
• template-generated
• card-heavy
• dashboard-like
• childish
• overly animated
• dependent on badges/pills
• like a standard booking portal

Luxury comes from restraint.

Use:
• whitespace
• typography
• hierarchy
• photography
• composition

Do NOT try to communicate luxury through:
