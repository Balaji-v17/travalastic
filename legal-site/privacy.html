2. Data We Collect and Store

We only collect personal data necessary to provide personalized travel planning, AI assistance, flight reservations, and expense management. We store the following categories of data in our cloud database (hosted on MongoDB Atlas):

2.1 Account Identity & Authentication

  • Data fields: Full name, registered email address, password hash (cryptographically hashed using bcrypt; plaintext passwords are never stored or accessible), and account creation timestamp.
  • Purpose: Creating your account, authenticating login sessions, securing access, and verifying ownership.
  • Retention & Deletion: Retained while your account remains active. Permanently deleted immediately upon account deletion.

2.2 Travel Itineraries, Preferences & Packing Templates

  • Data fields: Free-text travel prompts (rawRequest), destination, travel dates, budget tier (budget, mid, luxury), travel interest tags, structured daily activity schedules (activity titles, timing, notes, completion status, coordinates), cost estimates, AI reschedule undo history snapshots (editHistory), and custom packing templates (PackingTemplate).
  • Purpose: Generating day-by-day itineraries, tracking trip health, enabling undo/restore of AI modifications, and preparing packing checklists.
  • Retention & Deletion: Retained while your account is active. Permanently deleted when you delete your account.

2.3 AI Assistant Conversations: Trip Chat vs. Travel Assistant

Our platform provides two distinct AI assistants with different storage characteristics:

  • Trip Chat Assistant (Itinerary Grounded): Free-text chat questions and assistant answers about your specific trips are stored in our database in the conversations collection. This history is maintained so the assistant has context to answer follow-up trip questions. It is permanently erased when you delete your account.
  • General Travel Assistant (Stateless): General travel inquiries submitted to the Travel Assistant are processed statelessly. Message content and assistant replies are held strictly in temporary client/server memory during request execution. They are not stored in any database collection and are not logged in server logs.

2.4 Travel Expenses & Multi-Currency Splits

  • Data fields: Expense descriptions, amount in Indian Rupees (INR), original transaction amount and currency code, foreign exchange conversion rate, expense categories, and companion split details.
  • Purpose: Logging on-trip expenditures, converting foreign expenses to INR using reference exchange rates, and calculating split balances among travel companions.
  • Retention & Deletion: Stored until you delete the expense or delete your account.

2.5 Flight Bookings & Disruption Guardian

  • Flight Bookings: When you purchase flights through our integration with Duffel, we store flight reservation details: airline PNR reference, Duffel order ID, flight route, departure and arrival times, total fare, and passenger traveler details (passenger full names, honorific titles, genders, dates of birth, contact email, and contact phone).
  • Disruption Guardian (Airline Schedule Changes): When airlines notify us of flight schedule changes via Duffel webhooks, we record the disruption in our disruptions collection: delay minutes, original and rescheduled flight times, and AI reschedule proposals. Webhooks contain only flight timing changes; passenger personal identities are never received, extracted, or stored from webhooks.
  • Retention & Deletion: Stored while your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account.

Public Destination Catalogs: We also maintain curated reference databases of hotels (curatedhotels), transit routes (curatedroutes), tourist attractions (touristplaces), and destination search embeddings (destinationcontents). These contain publicly available travel information and no personal data.

3.1 External Partner Redirect Links

Our application includes optional redirect links that direct you to external service providers. When you tap these links, you leave Travalastic and interact directly with the partner; their respective privacy policies govern your use:

  • Uber: When you initiate cab booking from the app, pickup coordinates (from device GPS), dropoff coordinates, and destination address are forwarded to the Uber app or website.
  • RentalCars: Tapping rental car options forwards the destination city name to RentalCars.com.
  • Google Maps: Tapping directions for an activity forwards the activity coordinates (latitude, longitude) to Google Maps.
  • IRCTC & RedBus: Static links to train and bus portals. No personal or travel data is appended.

4. On-Device Storage & Device Permissions

4.1 Device Permissions

  • Foreground Location (Mobile): Requested only when booking a cab via expo-location to determine your pickup point. Coordinates remain in volatile device memory and are never sent to or stored on Travalastic backend servers.

4.2 On-Device Storage

  • Mobile Keystore (SecureStore): On iOS and Android devices, your active JSON Web Token (JWT) session credential is stored securely using expo-secure-store, backed by the operating system's hardware keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). It is erased upon logout or account deletion.
  • Mobile Memory Isolation: Travel Assistant conversation history exists only in volatile memory on the mobile device. It is not saved to disk and is purged when you clear chat, log out, or delete your account.
  • Web Storage (localStorage): In web browsers, the session token is stored in browser localStorage. No persistent cookies or sessionStorage are utilized.

5. Logging and Security Practices

We enforce privacy-preserving logging controls and data protections verified in our application code:

  • Zero Plaintext Passwords: Passwords are cryptographically hashed with bcrypt before storage.
  • Server Log Protection: Server-side HTTP request loggers (Morgan) log only request methods, URLs, status codes, and response times. Request bodies, passwords, payment details, and chat messages are never recorded in server logs.
  • Travel Assistant Privacy: Travel Assistant messages are not stored in any database and are not logged in server logs.
  • Trip Chat Retention: Trip Chat conversations are stored in our database to maintain trip context and are deleted when the user deletes their account.
  • LLM Tracing Disabled: External prompt tracing services (e.g., LangSmith) are not enabled.
  • Encrypted Transport: All data in transit between clients, backend services, and third-party APIs is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS.
  • Authorized Staff Access & Operational Auditing: Authorized staff access limited account data solely for customer support assistance and platform content management, with every administrative action recorded in an immutable audit trail.
