2. Data We Collect

We only collect and store data necessary to generate itineraries, facilitate bookings, track expenses, and manage your account. Based on our application architecture, we store the following data fields:

2.1 Account Information

  • Full Name: Your name as entered during account registration.
  • Email Address: Your personal email address used for login and account notifications.
  • Password Hash: A cryptographically hashed representation of your password created using bcrypt. We never store or have access to your plaintext password.
  • Account Creation Timestamp: The date and time your account was created.

2.2 Itineraries and Travel Plans

  • Travel Request & Destination: Raw text prompts describing your trip desires, destination city or region, and trip start/end dates.
  • Travel Preferences: Budget tier preference (budget, mid, or luxury) and selected interest tags (e.g., culture, adventure, food).
  • Generated Schedules & Activities: Structured daily plans, including activity names, descriptions, suggested timing, place identifiers, and geocoded geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude).
  • Cost Estimates: Total estimated budget and estimated daily costs in INR.
  • Accommodation Suggestions: Recommended hotels or lodging options tailored to your itinerary.
  • Packing Lists: Categorized packing suggestions generated for your scheduled activities and destination.

2.3 Conversation and Chat History

  • Chat Messages: Free-text messages you submit during trip planning and assistant responses generated by our system, linked to specific itineraries with timestamps.

2.4 Trip Expenses

  • Expense Records: Free-text expense descriptions, amounts in INR, spending categories (Food, Transport, Accommodation, Activities, Shopping, Other), and creation timestamps linked to your itineraries.

2.5 Flight Bookings and Passenger Information

  • Booking References: Duffel order identifiers, airline booking reference codes (PNRs), flight offer IDs, and booking status.
  • Flight Details: Airline carrier names, origin and destination airport codes, departure and arrival timestamps, flight durations, and segment details.
  • Passenger Traveler Data: Passenger given names, family names, titles (e.g., Mr, Ms), genders, dates of birth, contact email addresses, and phone numbers.
  • Transaction Amounts: Total payment amounts and currency codes.
  • Booking Payload: Raw order responses from Duffel containing flight tickets and document details.

Note on public catalog data: Our system also maintains curated catalogs of hotels, transit routes, destination content embeddings, and tourist landmarks. These collections contain public destination information and do not include user personal data.

3. How Your Data Is Used

We process the collected data strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Personalized Itinerary Generation: To generate multi-day travel schedules, activities, accommodation ideas, and packing lists tailored to your destination, travel dates, budget, and interests.
  • Interactive Assistance: To process your natural language questions, modification requests, and chat interactions regarding your travel plans.
  • Flight Search and Booking: To query real-time flight availability, process booking orders through our airline aggregator, and display booking confirmations.
  • Expense Management: To record and categorize your on-trip expenditures and calculate spending totals against your itinerary's budget estimate.
  • Account Management & Authentication: To verify your credentials, maintain authenticated sessions, and protect account ownership.

4.1 External Deep Links

Our mobile application provides optional deep links that open third-party applications or websites installed on your device:

  • Uber: When you choose to book a cab, your pickup coordinates (latitude, longitude), dropoff coordinates, and destination name are passed to Uber.
  • RentalCars: When browsing rental cars, your destination name is passed to RentalCars.
  • Google Maps: When tapping "Get directions" for an itinerary activity, the activity coordinates (latitude, longitude) are passed to Google Maps.
  • IRCTC & RedBus: Static links to train and bus booking portals; no personal or trip data is appended.

5. On-Device Data and Permissions

5.1 Device Permissions

  • Foreground Location: The app requests foreground location permission (via expo-location) only when you initiate cab booking to pre-fill your pickup point. Your device GPS coordinates remain strictly on your device in temporary memory; they are never sent to or stored on Travalastic backend servers.

5.2 Local Storage

  • Secure Credential Storage: On iOS and Android devices, your active session token (JWT containing your user ID and email) is stored using expo-secure-store, which is backed by the operating system's hardware-backed keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • Web Storage: If accessing via web browsers, the session token is stored in browser localStorage.
  • No Unencrypted App Storage: The application does not use AsyncStorage.

7. Security Measures

We employ security controls that are verified and enforced in our application codebase:

  • Password Hashing: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using bcrypt prior to database storage. Plaintext passwords are never stored.
  • Token-Based Authentication: Sessions are secured with JSON Web Tokens (JWT), with authentication enforced on private API routes.
  • Hardware-Backed Device Storage: Mobile tokens are stored in operating system keystores (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • Ownership-Scoped Queries: All database deletion and retrieval queries strictly filter by the authenticated user's ID to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Server Log Protection: Server-side HTTP loggers do not record request bodies; passwords, password hashes, auth tokens, and free-text chat contents are not written to server logs.
  • Encrypted Transport: Network communication between the mobile client and backend servers is encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.
