What Is Permanently Deleted

Executing the deletion flow immediately and permanently deletes the following records from our database:

  • Your User Account: Profile details, full name, registered email address, and hashed password credentials.
  • Itineraries: All saved and generated travel itineraries, destinations, daily schedules, activity notes, and undo history snapshots.
  • Conversations: All stored trip chat message history. (Note: general Travel Assistant messages are not stored in any database).
  • Expenses & Splits: All logged spending records, multi-currency conversion values, and bill split allocations.
  • Flight Bookings: Flight booking records and passenger identities linked to your user account stored in our database.
  • Packing Templates: All reusable custom packing templates and checklists.
  • Flight Disruptions: All recorded airline flight schedule changes, delay minutes, and reschedule proposals.
  • Device Session Tokens: Tokens in mobile SecureStore and web localStorage are invalidated and removed.

What May Remain After Deletion

  • Airline & Aggregator Ticketing Records: Flight ticket reservations already issued by airlines and Duffel are held by them under airline passenger transport regulations and their independent privacy notices. Account deletion in Travalastic does not cancel active airline tickets or erase airline ticketing systems.
  • Third-Party AI Ephemeral Logs: Prompts previously sent to AI providers (Groq, Cerebras, Google Gemini) are governed by their respective API retention terms.
